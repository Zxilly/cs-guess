Counter-Strike 2 battle result sound effects

These files were extracted from a locally installed copy of Counter-Strike 2
for browser playback. The battle result files reproduce the layers used by:

- Player.DeathHeadShotArmor.AttackerFeedback (battle-headshot-win.mp3)
- Player.DeathHeadShot.Victim (battle-headshot-loss.mp3)

Source assets:

- sounds/player/headshot_armor_e1.vsnd
- sounds/player/headshot_armor_flesh.vsnd

The final-ten-second countdown is the original audio used by the default
Counter-Strike 2 music kit:

- sounds/music/valve_cs2_01/bombtenseccount.vsnd
  (bomb-ten-second-countdown.mp3)
